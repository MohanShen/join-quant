## Correction (appended after the fact)

The `enhance` row above says the loop FAILED. **It did not.** The daily pipeline bounded the
stage at a hard-coded 60 minutes — a plausible ceiling while the day's budget was 55, and wrong
once VIP raised it to 170. The loop was healthy, 27 minutes into its 170, and was cut off at
exactly 60:00. `spawnSync bash ETIMEDOUT` is *our* kill, reported as the child's crash.

Worse, the kill reached only the wrapper: `claude -p --resume` survived as an orphan and was
still running an hour later, spending backtest minutes with nothing accounting for them and
holding the JQ session the pipeline believed it had released.

Both are fixed — `STAGE_TIMEOUT_MIN` now derives from `USAGE_LIMIT`, dispatch goes through
`scripts/with-timeout.sh` which kills the process *group*, and a cutoff is now reported as
`timeout`, not `error`. This file is left as written; the row above is what the run recorded.
