The flow

Solid boxes are executables; dashed boxes are durable stores. The badge on each stage is its skill entry point.

01Discover
listV2 crawl→ strategy-discover→ discovered.json resources.json no skill · cron only

Splits posts into strategies (32-char backtestId) and research write-ups. Nothing triggers screening afterwards — a human runs it.

02Screen
screen-prefilter→ post-screener ×N→ screen-merge→ verdicts.json /run-screen

Ranks on marginal information, not pass-likelihood. Blind-tested at 0.90 AUC against 0.66 for popularity.

03Normalize
pending-normalize→ strategy-normalize→ normalize-train.tsv→ normalize-sync no skill · cron only

The only stage that spends backtest minutes at scale. normalize-sync now also stubs wiki pages, prunes the queue, and refreshes family pages.

04Knowledge base
kb-stub→ wiki/strategies→ wiki-family-build→ wiki/families→ wiki-type-build→ wiki/types /ingest-strategy · pages only

Family and type builders have no skill of their own. family-match proposes a family for each new member but never writes one — 4 pages await confirmation.

Factor-board numbers are a hypothesis source: a different bench (zz500 / 3y / JQ's cost model), so they may never enter a family page, type page or candidate. A factor earns a ledger row only by being built and measured here. 量化课堂 is methodology, not material — it belongs upstream in study.

05Research
understand+ improve→ gatekeeper→ executor→ recorder→ family §2 / §6 · edge: /run-family

ONE loop over one lineage: why it works and whether it can be better share a single ranked queue, because they were the same machine. 無選擇壓力 is scoped to the IDEA TYPE, so a negative understand result is a finding, not a failed iteration. Finalises once on VAL — one validation per (family, epoch), enforced in the executor. Replaced the separate Study and Enhance stages, which could starve each other over the same 13 families.

06Components
backtest-series→ data/series · 369→ component-scan→ components.tsv research · integrate

Ranks members by what they add to the type leader, not by standalone score — the only layer that can see correlation.

07Integrate
wiki/types→ candidate.py→ type-integrate-check→ 整合回合 /run-integrate

Gate is sharpe 1.5, the same as every other stage since epoch 7. The bar that actually binds is per-year sharpe domination (epoch 8): the candidate must beat the best parent CHAMPION's sharpe in every TRAIN year. Beat the best member, not the gate. Six rounds run; the two universes with an eligible family are SEEDED (全A, 混合) and no merge has beaten its parents yet — 小盘 and 全A both REJECT.

08Out-of-sample

What still breaks

Eligibility, not integration, is the binding constraint — but it now feeds back throughput

What binds is the /run-family EXIT GATE: a champion on the live bench, a VAL that validated that book, and realizability ≥ 2. Only 2 of 13 families clear it, so most universes read IDLE … the gate is upstream.

On 2026-10-17 that stopped being a dead end. Every gate verdict now names the owner of its block — research / normalize / human / none — and family-queue reopens the research-owned ones as reason: integrate-blocked, carrying the gate's own sentence. 8 of 13 families were blocked here while the research queue read 0 due; 4 are now dispatchable (七星高照, 五福闹新春, 多因子ML, 趋势技术), each needing one VAL run. human and none are deliberately never reopened.

Discovery never triggers screening manual seam

strategy-daily and /run-screen share copy-queue.json, but nothing hands over. A human decides when to screen, and 8 strategies sit pending normalization behind that.

Family assignment stays human precision floor

Code matching decides only 31 of 175 labelled pages, and its abstain rules were tuned on those same 31 — so its precision is not an independent estimate. It proposes; a person promotes.

Tutorials left unwired by decision

量化课堂 teaches how to do factor research. Integration combines already-measured artefacts, so the fit is upstream — the study loop or a future research pipeline — not here.

Factor board is mid-cap, the exhausted types are small-cap transfer risk

The snapshot is zz500 while 小盘-H-unknown and 小盘-H-high are where new material is needed. IC may not transfer. Re-ingest --universe zz1000 before trusting it there.
